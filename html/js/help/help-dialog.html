Welcome to CleepDesktop!
The middle panel lists your Cleep devices. Devices on the local network are discovered automatically once they have started.

Devices appear in one list, sorted by name. Remote-access providers (when configured on the device) are shown next to the hostname.

Status icons:
  • offline (powered off or disconnected)
  • remote (reachable via the house gateway)
  • online
  • online with authentication enabled
Use the menu on a device for Device infos, Reload, Refresh remote URL, and Delete.
The right panel shows the current page: homepage, device configuration, install, monitoring, updates, help, and more.
